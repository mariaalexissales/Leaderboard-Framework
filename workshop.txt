title=Player Leaderboard System
description=[h1]Player Leaderboard System[/h1]
description=[h2]Made for MP Servers in mind :D[/h2]
description=[b]A server-wide kill leaderboard, and everyone hears about it when the crown changes hands.[/b]
description=
description=Press [b]Right-Ctrl[/b] and there it is. Hold it to peek, tap it to pin it open.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What It Tracks[/h2]
description=[b]Zombie kills[/b] and [b]player kills[/b], each its own board, each switched on or off in sandbox options.
description=
description=Every board comes in two flavours, picked from the right of the tab row. [b]All Time[/b] is the running total, so dying doesn't wipe your standing. [b]Per Life[/b] is the most you've ever managed on a single life - a personal record, and it stands after the death that ended the run. One rewards the long haul, the other rewards the run you're on right now.
description=
description=[b]The server hears about it when it matters.[/b] Take first place and everyone knows. Cut down the player holding the top Per Life spot and everyone knows that too - and when a run good enough to place in the top three ends in a horde, chat gets a send-off for it.
description=
description=[b]Everyone scores, staff included.[/b] If your admins spawn things in to test them, switch on [i]Ignore Debug-Mode Kills[/i] in sandbox options and anything killed while running the game in debug mode stays off the boards.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Where Your Scores Live[/h2]
description=Scores sit in the world save like any other mod data - in singleplayer that is your save folder, and on a server it is the save on whichever machine runs the server.
description=
description=[b]In singleplayer the boards use your character's name[/b] - the one on the character sheet. On a server they use the account, so your standing follows you through every character you lose.
description=
description=[h2]Chat Commands[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]/pls me[/b] - your own row, open to everybody
description=[*] [b]/pls dump[/b] - what the table holds [i](staff)[/i]
description=[*] [b]/pls dump <name>[/b] - one player's scores and the run they are on [i](staff)[/i]
description=[/list]
description=No debug mode and no console access needed - the answers come back in your own chat window.
description=
description=[b][u]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3508537032]NeatUI Framework[/url][/u][/b] by Rocco & Afyrmo - subscribe to that as well or the panel won't load.
